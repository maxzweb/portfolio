Blum.io Cases 2025 Gamification

Blum levels redesign that improved subscription conversion.

Product 1 product manager
Analytics 1 product analyst
My role Product designer responsible for levels UX/UI
Challenge: Users didn’t understand how levels worked, why they mattered, or how they affected the subscription — and conversion suffered.

What we saw in the data

A large share of users dropped off one or two steps before subscribing. Interviews showed people didn’t understand what levels gave them or how far they had progressed.

Hypothesis

If we make the level system transparent and tie it to concrete value (bonuses, limits, perks), users can decide on a subscription more easily.

What we changed

Simpler progress logic, a clear visual level path, and plain descriptions of what unlocks at each stage.

We emphasized actions that move users forward: completing tasks, trading, and engaging with the product.
