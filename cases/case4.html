Yandex Vzglyad Research B2B

Research template design for Yandex Vzglyad

Context

The business wanted to launch research faster and spend less analyst time explaining “what exactly we need.”

Templates as a product

We mapped typical business tasks and turned them into ready-made research scenarios with a clear structure, questions, and expected outcomes.

How it works

The client picks a task (e.g. creative testing or concept validation), gets a template with questions and structure, and sees what output they’ll receive.

Business value

Businesses get a fast start on research without extra calls and emails, while the team can focus on complex requests instead of repetitive ones.
